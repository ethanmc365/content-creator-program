controlled and headless - the heading, the paragraph and the button are gone

trip from the edit form; the full-size photo opens above the trip sheet
(PhotoLightbox is a portal); the map's route card is the trip rather than a line
of metadata; the community map names who flies a route (`route_creators`,

No wordmark, and it lifts away. **The white-then-orange flash was Vite's
render-blocking stylesheet**, not anything in `index.html`; `vite-boot-css.js`
turns it into a preload and `main.jsx` promotes it before rendering.
*Verified on the production URL: the CSS link carries `data-app-css`, the root
background is brand orange, the app renders styled.*

### Travel Games

three address tokens). The keyboard helper follows the keyboard instead of
sampling it twice, and the home-indicator inset is standalone-only.
*Verified at 375px: the language game opens with its header at the top, the
country game fits one screen, a guess submits and lists.*

## Still open

  share graphics still build ONE podium and ONE leaderboard from the flat
  ranking, so sharing a two-group challenge shares a board nobody competed on.
  It needs the same `boards` treatment `lib/winners` got. Nothing is wrong on
  screen; only the shared PNG is.

  not group-aware. That is probably correct - it ranks a market, not a
  challenge - but nobody has thought about it with groups in mind.

## Things worth not relearning (in addition to the previous file's list)

- **A z-index inside a page can never beat a `createPortal` layer on the body.**
  Any ancestor transform starts a stacking context, and this app puts `Reveal`
  transforms everywhere. Go to the body; do not raise the number.
- **A render-blocking stylesheet means the browser paints NOTHING**, inline boot
  markup included. And the `media="print" onload=` trick is unavailable here:
  the CSP has no `'unsafe-inline'` for scripts.
- **`autocomplete="off"` is advice WebKit ignores** on any field its classifier
  reads as an address, and it reads the name, the label and the placeholder.
- **Two mechanisms placing the same page is one too many.** The one that needs
  no measurement is the one that cannot be wrong.
- **A trigger auto-seeds market template point rules onto every new challenge.**
  A fresh points challenge already has per_post + 5k/10k/50k before the form
  writes anything.
- **`recalc_challenge_points` is the admin wrapper and refuses anything outside
  `my_managed_scopes()`. The triggers call `recalc_challenge_points_internal`.**
  A rule that must fire for a CREATOR's action belongs in the internal one.
