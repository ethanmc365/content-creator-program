1In one page

What changes, for whom, and what it costs.

Who

VIP creators

Creators too big for the normal challenges, or who want a different structure. Today: the Spanish VIPs and one or two in Romania, run from a WhatsApp group.

How they earn

Paid by views, not by winning

€0.25 for every 1,000 views on qualifying videos (the CPM), plus monthly targets and bonuses. No prize places, no leaderboard luck.

Where they live

Their own section, inside the whole community

A VIP hub and VIP rooms only they (and the team) can see, while keeping Worldwide, Global rooms, DMs and the calendar like everyone else.

How they get paid

One invoice a month, drafted for them

At month end the platform totals each VIP's views, applies the CPM and any bonus, and drafts the invoice. The team approves, the creator is notified, it is paid.

What it costs, at €0.25 CPM

The historical Spain VIP challenges (Jan-May 2026, in the Challenge Log) paid roughly €0.08-0.40 per 1,000 views. €0.25 sits in the middle and is predictable for both sides.

2Principles

  • One platform, two programmes. VIP is a flag on a creator, not a second app. Everything shared (profile, rooms, DMs, calendar, games, portfolio, certificates) stays shared.
  • Separate money, separate rules. VIP earnings never mix with challenge prizes. A VIP cannot enter the normal challenges (or can, but is never ranked or paid in them - your call), so nobody competes against a creator with 50x their audience.
  • Reuse what already works. The automatic view tracker (TikTok, Instagram, YouTube, Facebook), the old-video check, the video tracker, invoices and payment details, the KPI tracker, surveys and notifications all exist. VIP plugs into them rather than copying them.
  • Every number is live and explainable. A VIP can always see this month's views, videos and projected payout, and how each figure was reached.
  • The month closes itself. Nobody should total views in a spreadsheet again.

3What a VIP sees

A /vip hub, in the creator's language (Spanish and Romanian first), reached from a VIP badge on their profile menu and a VIP tab on Worldwide.

This month, live

A big earnings counter (views x CPM), views so far, qualifying videos, days left, and a projection for the month based on pace.

My target

Progress towards the monthly target set for them (e.g. 4 videos and 300k views), with the same progress bar as the KPI tracker. Hitting it unlocks the target bonus.

Submit a video

The same submit flow as challenges (paste a link), filed to the VIP month. Status: tracking, qualified, not eligible (and why).

My videos

Each video with its thumbnail, views, views counted this month, and what it has earned. Tap for its view curve.

VIP board

This month's VIPs by views (opt-in names or first name + market), with the bonus prizes shown against each place.

Payouts

Every month's statement: views, CPM, bonuses, invoice status (drafted, approved, paid) and the PDF.

VIP rooms

A VIP room per language plus one VIP Global room, with the full chat standard (markdown, media, reactions, search). Replaces the WhatsApp group.

Briefs and campaigns

Optional: the team posts suggested angles, destinations or deals ("film the Lisbon deal this week") with a CPM boost for videos on that brief.

Still part of the whole community: VIPs keep Worldwide, the Global rooms, DMs, the calendar and everything else, so they can connect with every creator. Their VIP badge shows on their profile.

4Videos and views

VIP posts on TikTok / IG / YouTube / FB→Submits the link→Checks: posted this month, mentions Tryp.com, public→View tracker updates every few hours→Month closes: views frozen→Invoice drafted
  • Reuse the challenge machinery by giving each month its own hidden "VIP month" record. Recommended: a vip_months row per month per programme, and VIP videos stored as submissions with a vip_month_id instead of a challenge. That keeps the existing view-sync, posted-at decoding, disqualify/reinstate and video tracker working unchanged.
  • Which views count decide: recommended views gained during the month on videos posted in the last 60 days. Snapshots at the start and end of each month give the "gained this month" figure, so a video that keeps growing earns again next month, and nothing is paid twice.
  • The old-video check already rejects videos posted before the window. Duplicates (same video submitted twice, or reposts) are blocked by the one-video-one-entry trigger.
  • Instagram and Facebook are read by the crawler route that already works; if a platform stops returning views, the tracker's health alert already flags it on the admin desk.

5How earnings are worked out

The formula

Payout = (qualifying views ÷ 1,000) × CPM + bonuses, rounded to the cent, capped at the creator's monthly cap if one is set.

6Targets and bonuses

Target bonus

Each VIP gets a monthly target (videos and/or views) set by the team. Hit it: +€X, or CPM x1.2 for the month.

Top of the month

Most views among VIPs: €100 / €50 / €25 for 1st-3rd (configurable per month).

Best single video

The month's most-viewed VIP video: a flat bonus and a spot on the wall of fame.

Consistency streak

Posted at least N qualifying videos in each of the last 3 months: +10% on the month.

Campaign boost

Videos on a team brief (a deal, a destination) earn a boosted CPM for that video only.

Milestones

First 1M views as a VIP, first €500 month: a certificate (the certificate system already exists) and a shout-out in the VIP room.

Bonuses are rules, not manual payments: each one is a row with a kind, amount and window, like challenge point rules, so the month-end close can apply them without anyone deciding by hand. Manual adjustments stay possible, with a reason, on the invoice draft.

7The month-end close and invoices

  1. Last day of the month, 23:59 in the creator's market time: the tracker takes a final views snapshot.
  2. 1st of the month, 06:00: a cron job computes each VIP's statement (views, CPM, tier, bonuses, cap) and drafts an invoice in the existing invoices system, one line per item, using their saved payment details.
  3. The admin VIP close screen shows every draft side by side, flags anything unusual (a spike, a creator with no payment details, a video disqualified late) and approves in bulk.
  4. Approved invoices go through the normal flow: the creator is notified (push + in-app, email once the mail domain is live), the PDF is on their Payouts page, and it is marked paid when the transfer is made.
  5. Late view corrections after the close go to next month as an adjustment line, never by reopening a paid month.

8Admin tools

VIP members

Add or remove a VIP, set CPM, cap, target and market, see their lifetime views and earnings. Moving a creator to VIP is one press from their profile.

This month

Live board of every VIP: views, videos, projected payout, target progress, total projected spend against budget.

Bonus rules

Set this month's bonuses and campaign boosts; preview what they would cost on current numbers.

Close the month

Drafted statements, flags, bulk approve, export CSV for finance.

KPI tracker

A "VIP programme" scope in the existing KPI tracker: views, effective CPM, active VIPs, spend, videos.

Analytics

VIP vs challenge creators: cost per 1,000 views, views per creator, retention month to month.

10Rules, quality and fraud

  • Qualifying video: posted within the window, public, on the creator's own verified account, mentions or tags Tryp.com (as the challenges already require), not a repost of an earlier video.
  • Automatic flags on the close screen: views jump more than 5x overnight, very low likes-to-views ratio, a video deleted after being counted, an account not on the creator's profile.
  • Disqualify and reinstate work as they do for challenges (the row moves out and back, totals recompute).
  • Written VIP terms (payment timing, what counts, bought views = removal) accepted in-app once, versioned.
