## Data definitions

Per-creator costs combine the reward ledger with earned participation and extra prizes in challenge standings, deduplicated by creator, challenge and prize slot. Estimated/unissued prizes are labelled as earned rather than paid. The imported challenge history contains aggregate totals and no creator identities; it cannot honestly be assigned to individual creators.

Challenge outreach distinguishes app activity from evidence that a creator read the challenge. It offers a reviewable notification and copyable email recipients/message, following the platform's existing manual email workflow. Testing must not send messages to real creators.

## View totals across analytics

The period overview previously summed the lifetime views of entries submitted during the dates. Weekly views reads actual view gains during those dates, including older entries. Both now use the same admin_views_gained RPC, inclusive day boundaries, and shared 60-second cache. The community overview names Community views gained, shows the VIP/combined totals beside it, and uses community gains in its cost-per-thousand denominator. The month comparison uses the same definition. This avoids claiming that community-only programme costs bought VIP views.
