# 100 things to build next

Written 13 Aug 2026, after the maps / chat / motion pass.

Sorted by what each one is FOR, not by size. A rough effort tag is on every
line — **S** is a day or less, **M** is a few days, **L** is a fortnight or
more, **XL** is a project. Where an idea depends on something that does not
exist yet, the dependency is named.

The platform already has: challenges, rooms, DMs and group DMs, the collab
board, connections, the creator map, milestones, games, referrals, invoices,
markets, resources, events. Nothing below repeats those; each is either a gap
in one of them or a new reason to open the app.

---

## A. Getting creators to know each other (1–14)

The whole product rests on this. Forty-five people who have never spoken are a
mailing list, not a community.

1. **Weekly intro pairing** — an opt-in that DMs two creators who have not
   spoken, with one shared thing to talk about (same market, same platform,
   overlapping travel). Runs every Monday. The single highest-leverage thing on
   this list. **M**
2. **"Ask the community" board** — a question with a title, tags and answers,
   distinct from chat. Chat answers are lost in a week; this is searchable
   forever and is what somebody planning a trip actually wants. **L**
3. **Answer credit on profiles** — "answered 14 questions", visible, so being
   generous is a status you can see. Needs #2. **S**
4. **Country expert badge** — automatic: lived there, or 2+ answered questions
   about it. Surfaces on the country panel the maps now open. **S**
5. **Who to meet this week** — three faces on the home page, chosen by shared
   destination, overlapping trip dates or the same platform, with the reason
   written on the card. Not "suggested"; explained. **M**
6. **Introduce two people** — a button on a profile that opens a group DM with
   both and a one-line reason. Warm intros beat cold ones every time. **S**
7. **Voice notes in DMs and rooms** — 60 seconds, waveform, no transcription.
   The fastest way to sound like a person instead of a username. **M**
8. **Reply threads in rooms** — a message with 12 replies currently swamps a
   room. Threads keep the main room readable. **M**
9. **Room-level unread counts and a "catch up" jump** — the rooms know what you
   have read (channel_reads) and never say so. **S**
10. **@here vs @everyone** — @everyone already notifies the whole network; a
    room-scoped @here would get used ten times as often and annoy nobody. **S**
11. **Local time on a profile** — "3:41pm for them" next to the DM button.
    Stops half the messages that never get answered. **S**
12. **Shared languages surfaced first** — the directory filters on language but
    never says "you both speak Portuguese" where it would matter. **S**
13. **Creator "office hours"** — a creator marks two hours a week as open for
    questions; it appears on their profile and the calendar. **M**
14. **Alumni status** — a creator who leaves keeps a read-only profile and their
    entries. People come back. Deleting them says they were staff. **S**

## B. The maps, now that countries are clickable (15–24)

15. **City panel to match the country panel** — tapping a town gives the roster;
    it should give the same treatment: who lives there, who has been, DM. **S**
16. **"I'm going here" from the country panel** — one tap from "nobody has been
    to Sudan yet" to a collab-board trip. The empty state currently offers a
    generic Plan a trip. **S**
17. **Country pages** — a real page per country: who lives there, who has been,
    the questions asked about it, the videos filmed there, the deals. The panel
    is the teaser; this is the destination. **L**
18. **Heat by content, not by people** — tint a country by how many challenge
    entries were filmed there. A different and more useful map. **M**
19. **Trip overlap alerts on the map** — two planes converging on the same
    country in the same fortnight should say so, not just both be drawn. **M**
20. **Filter the map by language, platform or market** — the directory filters
    the grid; the map ignores them. **S**
21. **Draw the route, not just the arc** — a trip with three stops is three
    hops. The collab board already stores them. **M**
22. **Past-travel replay** — a creator's own map animating their year, as a
    shareable video. The single most shareable thing this platform could make.
    **L**
23. **"Nobody has been here" leaderboard** — the twenty countries with no
    creator, as an invitation. Turns a gap into a challenge. **S**
24. **Offline-safe map tiles** — the TopoJSON is a megabyte from a CDN. Cache it
    in the service worker; the map is the slowest thing on the hub. **S**

## C. Challenges, which is where the money is (25–38)

25. **Draft submissions** — paste a link, come back later. Right now a
    submission is all-or-nothing at the moment of pasting. **S**
26. **Feedback on losing entries** — one line from the team on every entry that
    did not win. The cheapest retention lever there is. **M**
27. **Entry-to-entry comments** — creators critiquing each other's videos, opt
    in per challenge. **M**
28. **A brief that is a checklist** — "show the deal screen", "say the name",
    "under 30 seconds", ticked as you submit. Fewer disqualifications, less
    admin. **M**
29. **Team challenges** — two creators, one entry, prize split automatically.
    Forces collaboration, which forces connection. **L**
30. **Rolling monthly theme** with a weekly micro-brief inside it, so there is
    always something live. **M**
31. **Auto-pull view counts** where the platform allows it, so the leaderboard
    is not a self-reported number. Check each platform's terms first. **L**
32. **"Why this won"** on every results page — three sentences. Teaches the
    standard better than any brief. **S**
33. **Second-chance pot** — a small prize drawn from everyone who entered but
    did not place. Keeps the quiet majority entering. **S**
34. **Challenge templates** for admins, so a new brief is a fill-in rather than
    a blank page. **M**
35. **Submission preview** — show the creator exactly what the card will look
    like on the leaderboard before they submit. **S**
36. **Deadline reminders that respect the timezone** — the deadline is local
    midnight; the reminder should be local evening. **S**
37. **Per-market prize pots** funded by that market's performance, so a Romanian
    creator is not competing against the UK's budget. **L**
38. **A public winners page** on tryp.com, indexed, with creator credit and a
    link out. Free distribution for them, free SEO for us. **M**

## D. Making a creator's work visible (39–50)

39. **Media kit export** — one PDF: reach, best entries, countries, languages,
    testimonials. Creators ask brands for this constantly. **M**
40. **Public profile page** at a real URL, opt-in, indexable. **M**
41. **Portfolio picks** — let a creator pin three entries as their best work
    rather than the platform deciding by views. **S**
42. **Before / after reach** — what their following did over their time in the
    programme. The single strongest recruitment asset we could have. **M**
43. **Testimonials from the team**, requestable, shown on the profile. **S**
44. **Year in review** — a personal, shareable recap in December. **M**
45. **Certificates for milestones**, as an image sized for a story. **S**
46. **A "hire me" toggle** that surfaces a creator to brands browsing the
    directory. Needs a brand-side view. **L**
47. **Rate card, private to admins**, so briefing a paid piece stops being a
    negotiation from zero. **M**
48. **Content calendar per creator** — what they plan to post and when, visible
    to the team, so campaigns can be timed around it. **L**
49. **Cross-promotion pairs** — two creators agree to feature each other; the
    platform tracks it and reports the lift. **M**
50. **Best-performing hooks library**, drawn from real entries that did well
    here rather than from generic advice. **M**

## E. Learning and getting better (51–60)

51. **Structured paths** in the library — "your first travel video", six
    resources in order, with progress. Resources today are a flat list. **M**
52. **Weekly teardown** — the team picks one entry and explains its first three
    seconds. Ships as a room post and a library entry. **S**
53. **Peer review requests** — "look at this before I post it", 24-hour window,
    two reviewers. **M**
54. **A gear and app list per creator**, because "what do you edit on" is asked
    weekly. **S**
55. **Guest sessions** — a monthly call with somebody outside the programme,
    recorded into the library. **M**
56. **Subtitle and caption templates** in the brand's type, downloadable. **S**
57. **A checklist of platform rules** per country (disclosure, ad labelling)
    which changes by market and nobody tracks. **M**
58. **Music licensing guidance** — the single most common way an entry becomes
    unusable. **S**
59. **Analytics literacy explainer** — what a 3-second view actually counts as
    on each platform. **S**
60. **"Ask me anything" archive** from past sessions, searchable. Needs #2. **S**

## F. Rhythm — reasons to open the app on a Tuesday (61–70)

61. **A daily thread that is different every day** — Monday plans, Wednesday
    wins, Friday questions. Automated, one room post. **S**
62. **Streaks for posting, not just for games** — the games have streak freezes;
    the community does not. **M**
63. **Birthday and anniversary cards** already exist for birthdays; add
    "one year in the programme". **S**
64. **A weekly digest email** — what happened, what is live, who joined. The
    email tooling exists; the digest does not. **M**
65. **Push for the things that matter** (challenge closing, DM, invite), and
    nothing else. Prefs already exist; the taxonomy is too coarse. **M**
66. **A "what's happening now" strip** on the home page — who is online, what
    was just posted. **S**
67. **Local meetups** — a market can propose one, people RSVP, it lands on the
    calendar. The Meetups rooms exist and are empty because there is no object
    to put in them. **L**
68. **Countdown to the next challenge**, not just the current one. **S**
69. **Room of the week** — surface a quiet room to the whole network once a
    week. **S**
70. **Time-boxed sprints** — 48-hour mini-challenges, no prize, just a
    leaderboard. Cheap and habit-forming. **M**

## G. Trust, safety and moderation (71–78)

71. **Report a message** — there is delete and mute for admins and nothing at
    all for creators. **M**
72. **Block a creator** — DMs are open by request; blocking is not possible.
    **M**
73. **Moderation queue** for reported content, with an audit trail. Needs #71.
    **M**
74. **Slow mode** on a room during a busy launch. **S**
75. **Edit window on messages** — two minutes, with an "edited" marker. **M**
76. **Message retention policy** per room, stated in the room. **S**
77. **Two-factor for admins** — the admin surface can move money now. **M**
78. **A public changelog of moderation decisions**, anonymised. Trust is built
    by showing the work. **S**

## H. Money, and making it obvious (79–86)

79. **A creator-facing earnings page** — everything earned, paid, pending, in
    one place. The invoice queue is admin-side. **M**
80. **Payment status notifications** — "approved", "sent", "paid". Currently
    silence between the two ends. **S**
81. **Multi-currency clarity** — show a Romanian creator their prize in lei with
    the rate and date used. **M**
82. **Referral rewards paid automatically** at the counted stage rather than by
    hand. **M**
83. **Tiered rewards** — consistent entrants earn a higher baseline. Rewards
    effort, not luck. **L**
84. **Affiliate links per creator** with real attribution, so posting a Tryp
    deal earns. Probably the biggest commercial idea here. **XL**
85. **Expense claims** for a briefed shoot, into the same invoice pipeline. **M**
86. **Tax guidance per market**, written once, linked from the payment page.
    **M**

## I. Growth and recruiting (87–92)

87. **Referral leaderboard**, public, with the reward shown. **S**
88. **Application via a video** rather than a form. Faster to judge, and it is
    the actual skill. **M**
89. **Waiting list per closed market**, so opening Poland starts with fifty
    people rather than zero. **S**
90. **A public "join" page per market** in that market's language, even though
    the community itself stays English. **M**
91. **Creator-of-the-month**, chosen by the community rather than the team.
    **S**
92. **Alumni referrals** — the people who left are the warmest source there is.
    Needs #14. **S**

## J. The platform itself (93–100)

93. **Search across everything** — messages, resources, creators, countries.
    Cmd+K searches commands, not content. **L**
94. **Offline-first message queue** — a message written in a tunnel should send
    when the signal returns. **L**
95. **Real-time presence in a room** ("three people here"), not just online
    dots. **M**
96. **A proper notification centre** with grouping and read state. **M**
97. **Accessibility pass** — keyboard paths through chat and the maps, focus
    order, contrast on the orange-on-orange states. **M**
98. **Performance budget in CI** — the initial bundle has crept back up; make a
    regression fail the build rather than be noticed six weeks later. **S**
99. **A staging environment with seeded data** — every UI bug in this session
    was found by hand on production data. **M**
100. **An admin "explain this number" mode** — click any statistic and see the
     query behind it. Analytics people trust get used. **M**

## If only five got built

1, 2, 17, 39, 84. Pairing and the questions board make the community a
community; country pages give the maps somewhere to lead; the media kit makes
the programme worth staying in; affiliate links make it worth paying for.
