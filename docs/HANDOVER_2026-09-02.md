NOT verified, and each for a reason:

- **The market live-challenge card's new leaderboard panel** and the orange
  deadline card. There is no live challenge in production right now, so neither

- **The group photo upload** and the DM/room skeleton overlay were not driven.

**A NOTE ON MEASURING ANYTHING IN THE PREVIEW PANE.** Three times this session a
correct layout read as broken: computed `width: 0px` against an inline
`width: 98.1px`, an identity `transform` against an inline `translate3d`, and
`opacity: 0` on a settled chat. The pane reports `visibilityState: 'hidden'`, so
CSS transitions and rAF do not advance and every animated property reads as its
START value. A screenshot forces a paint and everything lands. **Do not "fix" a
frozen animation measured in a hidden pane** - this file's predecessors have
paid for the rAF half of this three times already.

## The migrations

**164** `creator_photos.sort_order_mobile` / `size_mobile`. Two nullable
overrides, not two full sets: null means "use the desktop value", so every board
that existed keeps exactly the arrangement it had at both widths.

**165** `conversations.photo_url`. A group can wear a photograph instead of an
emoji; the photo simply wins over the emoji and the accent when there is one.

**166** `best_streak_leaderboard()`. The same gaps-and-islands query
`streak_leaderboard` uses, asked a different question: the longest run there has
ever been rather than the one ending today.

**167** `community_aircraft_gap()`. How many logged flights carry no aircraft.

**168 / 169** `public.translations` + `can_edit_locale()`, and
`communities.language` as the market's locale. 168 added a `locale` column
before noticing `language` already existed, unused; 169 removes it again.

## Profiles

**TWO PHOTO BOARDS, ONE TABLE** (migration 164). A phone and a desktop pack the
same photographs into genuinely different collages, and there was one stored
arrangement, so tidying one rewrote the other.

**THE SIZE LADDER STEPPED DOWN A NOTCH** and grew a genuinely smaller smallest.
That cannot be done on three columns - the old small was already one whole
column - so the GRID got finer: six columns on a desktop, four on a phone, with
the three levels at 1, 2 and 4 of them. New large is two thirds of the board
(the old medium), new medium is a third (the old small), new small is half that
again.

**THE DRAG IS SMOOTHER FOR TWO REASONS, AND NEITHER IS EASING.** A tile moves on
its `transform` now rather than on `left`/`top`, so ten tiles re-flow on the GPU
instead of running layout sixty times a second. And `dropIndex` has a DEAD BAND:
a finger resting within a pixel of a tile's centre line used to flip the answer
every frame, and every flip re-packed the whole board. That is what "all the
other ones were moving around" was.

**"TIDY THEM UP" ACTUALLY DOES SOMETHING NOW.** It cleared the four `pos_*`
columns, which have been dead since the packer took over - the drawn span comes
from `size`. It resets this variant's ladder to small and renumbers by upload
order.

**THE DATE OF BIRTH WAS NEVER THE SAVE.** `profiles` has a BEFORE trigger,
`mirror_dob_to_private`, which copies any dob into `creator_private.dob`,
derives `profiles.age` and then sets `new.dob := null` - by design. So the write
worked perfectly and the form re-seeded itself from `profiles.dob`, which is
null on every row in the database and always will be. It reads the private row
now. **And a rejected profile save now says so** - it was thrown away, so a
profile that would not write looked exactly like one that had.

**THE CLOCK IS A LABELLED CELL** like the home town and the age under it, with a
bare brand glyph instead of a tinted disc.

**THE FACE ON THE COUNTRIES MAP HOLDS ITS SIZE THROUGH A PINCH.** `onMoveEnd`
was the only thing writing `position`, so during a gesture the group was at the
new scale while React held the old one and the marker - whose job is to divide
that scale back out - grew with the map. It is written straight to the DOM from
`onMove` now; a `setPosition` per frame would re-render 177 country paths.

## Chat

**THE OPENING GLITCH HAD A CAUSE THE PREVIOUS FIX COULD NOT SEE.** A
zero-height `<img>` IS a stable height, so `pinToBottom` asked "has scrollHeight
stopped changing", got yes, revealed the thread, and was then shoved by however
many pictures landed afterwards in whatever order the network chose. That is the
"different every refresh".

Three things fix it: the **eight legacy room attachments had their real
dimensions measured and written** (they predate migration 163 and carried none);
`waitingOnMedia` makes "not started" different from "finished" for anything near
the viewport; and every attachment now reserves a box even with no stored shape
(a provisional 3:4, replaced the instant a real measurement arrives). The cap
went 700ms to 1200ms - 700 was landing inside the window a legacy attachment
needs to decode.

**THE THREAD NEVER SHOWS A BLANK ROOM.** `settled` hid the scroller and left a
white rectangle that then filled all at once, which is what "the text is
flashing" was. A bottom-aligned skeleton covers the gap, in the rooms and DMs.

**A REAL DOWNLOAD GLYPH.** `arrow-down` is a full-height bare arrow - the glyph
for "go down the page". Every save and export in the product uses the arrow into
a tray now.

**A GROUP CAN WEAR A PHOTOGRAPH** (migration 165), and the icon shortlist is a
six-column GRID rather than a wrap that came out ten-and-two at most widths.

**THE WAVING HAND FILLS ITS BOX.** The four motion flourishes lived at the
corners, so the hand had to be shrunk to 0.72 to leave room; without them it
takes 0.94 and the lean is what says "waving".

**NOTHING YOU TYPE IN SITS UNDER THE KEYBOARD** (`lib/keyboardFollow`, installed
once from AppLayout). Three reports - the schedule dialog's date and time, a
poll's options, a game challenge's title - were one missing behaviour. And a
dialog is now as tall as what you can SEE: `max-h-[90vh]` is measured against
the layout viewport, which does not shrink when a keyboard opens, so a sheet
went on claiming ninety percent of a screen whose bottom half was a keyboard and
its content had no reason to scroll.

**`useKeepAboveKeyboard` IS DELETED.** Two mechanisms scrolling one page is one
too many, and its first correction was a SMOOTH scroll issued while the keyboard
was still animating. That race is what "glitchy scrolls down a bit as I'm
typing" was.

## Spanish, and the editor

**THE TABLES WERE NEVER GOING THROUGH THE DICTIONARY.** The nav links and their
descriptions, the market names, the room labels, the game modes and blurbs, the
board's filters and the "who to meet" reasons were all printed straight off
const arrays. Every one of those is a VARIABLE at the call site, so
`npm run i18n:report` - which scans for `tr('literal')` - could not see any of
them, and they looked translated because the report said so. Read that report's
"in the dictionary, not seen at a call site" section as a WORKLIST.

**A MARKET NAME IS THE ONE EXCEPTION** to "nothing a person wrote is ever
translated". There are seven, we chose all seven, they are the names of
countries, and a Spanish creator being shown "UK & Ireland" in a Spanish
interface is the product half-translated. `lib/markets.marketName`.

**`/admin/languages`** (migration 168) is the editor. It is NOT under
`AdminRoute`: a market manager is not an admin and is the person this is for.
The page decides what to draw and the RLS policy decides what saves.

- `public.translations` is an OVERRIDE LAYER over `src/locales/es.js` on the
  same keys, fetched at boot beside the dictionary and merged at lookup. It
  never blocks and never throws: every failure resolves to the bundled file,
  which is a complete screen. Clearing a box DELETES the row rather than storing
  a blank, so the way back is the shipped word.
- `src/locales/catalogue.json` is generated by `prebuild` from the same scan the
  report uses, so "the Challenges page" is a real list of what that page says
  and nobody maintains it. Strings no screen claims - the tables above - land in
  an **Everything else** bucket, or they would be reachable only by search, by
  somebody who already knew they existed.

Still English, deliberately: the admin tools, ManageChapter, GlobalSettings, the
legal pages and the invoice paperwork.

## Worth not relearning

- **A CSS transition or an animation measured in a hidden pane reads as its
  START value.** See the note at the top. Screenshot first.
- **Animating a box's SIZE is animating layout.** Moving a tile to a transform
  and leaving `width`/`height` in the transition list keeps the expensive half
  of what you were removing.
- **A drop target with no hysteresis oscillates where the finger actually
  rests** - on the boundary it is aiming at.
- **A BEFORE trigger that nulls the column you just wrote is invisible from the
  client.** The write succeeds, the read comes back empty, and the form looks
  like it lost your edit. `pg_get_triggerdef` on the table before believing a
  save is broken.
- **The i18n report cannot see `tr(item.label)`.** Coverage measured only over
  literals will say a screen is finished while half of it is a table.
- **A silent save is worse than a failed one.** Two bugs this session were
  invisible because the error was thrown away.
